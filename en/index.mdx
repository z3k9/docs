Welcome to the user guide for **First Bank DRC's Corporate Internet Banking (CIB)** platform. CIB gives your business secure, self-service access to its accounts, payments, approvals, and administration.

Choose how you use CIB to get started.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Web Portal" icon="browser" href="/en/web-portal/sign-up">
    Sign up, set up your organisation, and run day-to-day banking from your browser.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mobile app" icon="mobile-screen" href="/en/mobile/overview">
    Check balances, move money, and approve transactions on the go.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Host-to-Host" icon="server" href="/en/host-to-host/overview">
    Connect your ERP or treasury system directly to the Bank over secure SFTP.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
